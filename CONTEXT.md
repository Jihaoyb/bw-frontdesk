# AI Front Desk

The AI Front Desk helps families get center-specific answers and helps operators improve the information available to families.

## Language

**Center**:
An early education provider whose policies and day-to-day operations are the subject of family inquiries.

**Enrolled family**:
A family with a child currently enrolled at the center. Enrolled families are the primary audience for this prototype.

**Prospective family**:
A family considering enrollment at the center.

**Operator**:
A staff member responsible for maintaining center information and reviewing questions the front desk could not adequately answer.

**Operator improvement loop**:
The process in which a family inquiry exposes a knowledge gap, an operator improves the available information, and subsequent inquiries benefit from that improvement.

**Published knowledge**:
Center information explicitly approved by an operator for reuse in answers to family inquiries.
_Avoid_: Answers bank

**Staff reply**:
An operator's response to a specific family inquiry. A staff reply does not become published knowledge unless an operator explicitly approves it for reuse.

**Pending request**:
A family inquiry awaiting operator review because the front desk needs staff input or confirmation.

**Staff handoff**:
The referral of an inquiry to an operator when published knowledge cannot support a sufficient answer or a requested action requires staff confirmation.

**Knowledge gap**:
Missing or inadequate published knowledge that prevents a sufficient answer to a family inquiry. Replying to an individual inquiry does not by itself close the knowledge gap.

**Inquiry history**:
The record of family questions, front desk answers, staff replies, and request outcomes.

**Knowledge validity**:
The period or circumstances in which published information applies. This is distinct from how long an inquiry remains in history.

**Knowledge entry**:
A separately maintained piece of center information with a title, policy text, and publication timestamp.

**Answer source**:
The published knowledge entry supporting an automated answer, with its exact policy text available to the family.

**Conversation**:
The persistent exchange of messages about a family inquiry, including automated answers and any staff follow-up.

**Staff request**:
A tracked need for staff input within a conversation. Its progress is distinct from whether a message was saved or read.

**Sensitive inquiry**:
A family message about an incident involving a child, a custody or pickup restriction, a billing dispute, a complaint about staff, or a health matter beyond published policy. The front desk does not answer it from policy; it goes to staff and is flagged in the inbox.

**Decision time**:
The time by which a parent says they need information to make a decision. It is not a promised staff response deadline.
