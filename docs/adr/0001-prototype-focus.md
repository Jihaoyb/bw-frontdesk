# Demonstrate the operator improvement loop for enrolled families

The prototype will demonstrate only the enrolled-family experience and show how a question that cannot be answered exposes a knowledge gap, how an operator improves the center's information, and how a subsequent question benefits. This prioritizes a coherent parent-and-operator experience over equal coverage of prospective and enrolled families or a broad collection of independent AI features. The three-hour implementation budget includes deployment, verification, and the submission explanation.
