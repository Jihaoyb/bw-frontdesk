# Cover the assignment's example questions and sensitive inquiries in the baseline

The take-home prompt lists five example parent questions (Veterans Day, infant tuition, a child with a fever, a forgotten lunch with "what is it," and scheduling a tour) and asks that uncertain or sensitive questions be handled gracefully, with an operator view of what is being asked and where the system struggled. A reviewer will type those five questions first. The seed and fixtures in [test inquiries](../test-inquiries.md) now carry all five verbatim: Veterans Day replaces the Friday after Thanksgiving as the deliberate knowledge gap, two small entries for tuition and tours are added so those questions get sourced answers rather than an awkward handoff, and the lunch row expects the assistant to say that today's menu is not in published knowledge. None of this widens the product scope in [ADR 0001](0001-prototype-focus.md); a prospective-family experience remains out of scope even though a tour entry exists.

Sensitive inquiries are a new interaction rule rather than a new feature. An incident involving a child, a custody or pickup restriction, a billing dispute, a complaint about staff, or a health matter beyond published policy is never answered from policy. The assistant acknowledges briefly, offers Ask staff at once or proceeds directly when the parent asks for staff, and the request is flagged sensitive in the Operator inbox with no knowledge-update draft suggested. This reuses the existing handoff path and a prompt rule, so it costs little and closes an explicit gap in the earlier plan.

Showing which questions were asked and where the assistant struggled is an explicit operator requirement, so it moves out of the deferrable matching-counts ticket. Every parent question, with or without a staff request, is persisted and listed in the Operator view with its outcome (answered, clarified, handoff offered, sensitive, failed) as part of the answer ticket; the matching-counts ticket keeps only the normalized count. The prompt frames three hours as an effort recommendation within a three-business-day window; the three-hour figure in this repository is a self-imposed hard cap on implementation time, kept deliberately so the budgets and cut lines in [ADR 0007](0007-isolate-human-prerequisites-and-budget-tickets.md) stay binding.
