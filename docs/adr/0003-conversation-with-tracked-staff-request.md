# Keep staff follow-up inside a persistent conversation

Parents and staff will communicate asynchronously in the same conversation, with an embedded request card tracking outstanding staff work separately from message delivery. This preserves context while making unresolved needs visible, without creating an expectation of live staff availability. A read event does not establish responsibility, and saving or reviewing a request does not confirm the requested service.

The required prototype includes the conversation and request card. An optional parent-selected decision time is the intended novelty extension and the first feature to cut under the three-hour budget; it is not a promised response deadline. External notifications, presence tracking, and automatic reassignment remain outside the prototype. The alternatives and research are recorded in [communication research](../communication-research.md).
