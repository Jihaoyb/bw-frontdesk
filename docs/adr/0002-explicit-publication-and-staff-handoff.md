# Require explicit publication before reusing staff replies

An operator's answer to an individual inquiry does not automatically become reusable center knowledge: operators must explicitly publish information for reuse. This preserves the distinction between a situational staff reply and an approved policy, at the cost of requiring an additional operator action to improve future automated answers.

The front desk can answer policy questions but refers inquiries requiring staff judgment or service confirmation to an operator through a visible pending request. It must not turn a general policy into a promise of a service or permission for a particular child to attend.

Operators can send a reply alone or send a reply and open an editable knowledge-update draft for explicit publication. Reply-only requests leave the knowledge gap visible. Operators can also add or update knowledge directly, independently of any inquiry. Repeated questions remain individual requests with a simple recurring-question count; the prototype does not automatically merge or bulk-answer them.

Fictional inquiry history is retained for the lifetime of the demo, without timed deletion. Retaining a staff reply does not make it eligible for reuse as published knowledge.
