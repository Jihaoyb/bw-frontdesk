# Production considerations

Inquiry retention and knowledge validity serve different purposes. In a real product, define a configurable inquiry-retention policy with centers based on their operational and privacy needs; do not invent a universal 3-, 10-, or 30-day lifetime. An unresolved request should remain visible until resolved or explicitly closed rather than silently expiring.

Published knowledge should support effective dates, optional expiration, and replacement by newer versions. Expired or superseded information should be excluded from current answers while prior versions remain available according to the retention policy. Time-sensitive requests, such as lunch for a particular day, should become visibly outdated and require re-confirmation rather than imply an ongoing commitment. These are production recommendations; date-validity controls are not yet committed prototype scope.
